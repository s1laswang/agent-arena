# Google DeepMind: Best Use of Gemini API (tech challenge)
Page: https://hackjunction.app/participate/45/tech-challenges/48. Open innovation.

**What they want.** Go beyond just adding an LLM. Show why Gemini is essential and what makes the use innovative or technically impressive. The submission must explain:
1. The problem and why it matters.
2. How the Gemini API is integrated.
3. What is innovative or impressive about it.
4. Why Gemini is essential.

**Prizes.** 1st: €4,000 plus $1,000 in Google Cloud credits per member. 2nd: €2,000. 3rd: €1,000.

**Gemini access.** Usage is effectively unlimited. GEMINI_API_KEY is in `.env`, which is gitignored.
