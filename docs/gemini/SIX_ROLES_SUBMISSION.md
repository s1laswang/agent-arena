## Platform Summary

Canary is a Bayesian insider-threat RL benchmark where an AI security lead plants canary traps to catch a leaking mole before company value bleeds away. Across 55 verified gemini-4-carbon runs (4,242 calls, 116.4M input tokens, 104.9M cached), Gemini powers six load-bearing roles: leak-free company text, solvability-gated adaptive moles, 13-tool frontier investigators across thinking levels, a 1M-context failure analyst with 100% verified quotes, a whole-repo red-team swarm, and pixel-watermark vision forensics. Every claim replays offline with no API key.

## 1. The Problem and Why It Matters

Organizations increasingly delegate high-stakes investigative workflows to AI agents: insider-threat triage, incident response, fraud review, and security operations. In each setting an agent must gather partial evidence under a tight action budget while damage compounds, then commit to an irreversible decision.

Conventional benchmarks compress agent evaluation into a single scalar score. That scalar cannot answer the three questions engineering teams need before deployment:
- **Where decisions break:** which specific action lost expected value relative to optimal Bayesian reasoning (`premature_accusation`, `uninformative_canary`, `ignored_delay`, `over_restriction`, or `budget_waste`).
- **What extra thinking buys:** whether scaling inference-time reasoning improves information design or commit timing, and at what token and latency cost.
- **Whether the benchmark itself is fair and ungameable:** whether difficulty comes from genuine reasoning gaps rather than impossible worlds, leaked ground truth, or exploitable reward loopholes.

Answering those questions manually requires reading hundreds of multi-day tool trajectories, designing catchable adversarial scenarios, inspecting visual evidence artifacts, and red-teaming the environment source code line by line. Canary automates the entire scientific loop on a single frontier model: `gemini-4-carbon`.

## 3. What Is Innovative and Technically Impressive

1. **Solvability-constrained adversarial co-evolution (`adaptive_mole`).** Rather than making scenarios harder through unfair noise, `gemini-4-carbon` evolves 49 mole DSL programs (`mole-forge-carbon-hard-deep`) and proposes PAIRED scenarios under a strict solvability gate: every candidate is rejected unless `SolverPolicy >= X` and `ObviousHeuristic <= Y`. On `v1.4` `hard` (`results/gemini_roles/adaptive_mole/paired-skill-designer-carbon-hard`, commit `0e59cc10aa43f068e5fe7c1007215df1db4d10fe`), `6/6` generated scenarios pass the gate (`SolverPolicy +0.628` vs `ObviousHeuristic -1.000`), and in head-to-head play (`results/gemini_roles/adaptive_mole/carbon-vs-carbon-seed0`, commit `3bd1d1e2295aa320812f08e2eceaef5b4a8902ef`), a `gemini-4-carbon` investigator defeats a live `gemini-4-carbon` mole (`+0.660` vs paired solver `+0.400`).
2. **Controlled thinking-to-competence decomposition (`investigators`).** By holding `gemini-4-carbon`, the tool definitions, and the world seeds fixed while toggling `thinking_level` (`low` vs `high`), we separate experimental design quality from stopping calibration. On `v1.4` `hard` seed `0` (`results/gemini_roles/investigators/thinking-ladder-carbon-hard0`, commit `3df4d2b65bd05a67f2d94ef18d967da05c0e90d7`), scaling from `0` to `52,968` thinking tokens eliminates `uninformative_canary` mistakes, raises canary posterior entropy from `1.1776` to `1.5038` bits (`SolverPolicy` `1.5335`), and slashes total solver regret by `81.6%` (`0.1623 -> 0.0298`), while exposing over-cautious accusation delay (`P=0.9984` on day `13` vs optimal break-even `1/(1+V)` on day `7`).
3. **Checked-not-trusted long-context forensics (`failure_analyst`).** `gemini-4-carbon` reads `101` complete trajectories (`596,862` tokens in `atlas-carbon` and `237,763` tokens in `carbon-longctx-corpus`) in one context window, but never computes regret or invents evidence: every regret number comes from `canary.solver.regret.attribute`, achieving `93%` solver agreement and `100.0%` character-for-character verified transcript quotes.
4. **Whole-repo self-auditing red team (`exploit_hunter`).** `gemini-4-carbon` reads `104` repository source files (`203,463` tokens) to hypothesize concrete seed-reconstruction, `/ledger` token, and validation exploits, then executes 655 combined tool calls across in-process and HTTP MCP boundaries. Every score is read strictly from the server-side ledger, confirming `0` seed leaks across `12,000+` `public_world_match` probes.
5. **Causal pixel-only and multi-image multimodal ablations (`multimodal_evidence`).** Across 9 verified vision runs (`115` PNG images read via `Part.from_bytes`), Canary tests causal pixel dependence: redacting text watermarks across 11 unique `medium` and `hard` seeds drops blind play to `+0.1845` pooled (`+0.0000` on `medium`, `+0.4060` on `hard`), while `gemini-4-carbon` vision decodes `100.0%` of copy-codes and recovers `+0.6700` (`100.0%` of the text oracle). On 10-world `v1.4` SOC rasters (`results/gemini_roles/multimodal_evidence/access-heatmap-v14-sweep-carbon`, commit `b40e5c8ddd303039e7fb605ec50b4005a174a064`, `90%` mole and `100%` decoy vs `0%` naive) and 4-panel `v1.4` trajectory charts (`results/gemini_roles/multimodal_evidence/carbon-trajectory-forensics`, commit `44b700f1b8d03f46eb90bc99142f4ffc221563cf`, `7/7` across all 5 forensic checks), vision turns raw pixels into calibrated Bayesian diagnostics.

## 4. Why Gemini Is Essential

Removing `gemini-4-carbon` from any of the six roles measurably degrades or disables the benchmark:
- **Without native multimodal vision (`Part.from_bytes`):** Redacted leak-memo investigations drop from `+0.6683` (`6/6` caught) to `+0.0000` (`0/6` caught) on `medium` and from `+0.6720` to `+0.4060` on `hard`, single-image baselines score `0/5` vs `5/5` on 3-image dossiers, and naive log heuristics pick the framed decoy `16/16` times across all SOC raster runs (`0%` mole accuracy vs `93.8%` with Gemini vision).
- **Without 1M-token long context:** Neither the `101`-episode trajectory corpus (`596,862` tokens in `atlas-carbon`, `237,763` in `carbon-longctx-corpus`) nor the `104`-file whole-repository security audit (`203,463` tokens) fits in a single unfragmented reasoning pass.
- **Without explicit thinking levels & thought signatures:** Multi-step 13-tool investigations lose cross-turn reasoning continuity, and controlled `low` vs `high` thinking ablations on identical model weights become impossible.
- **Without high-hit-rate context caching (`90.2%` cached across `116.4M` input tokens):** Running `4,242` multi-turn frontier agent and red-team calls would cost roughly 4x more, pricing iterative benchmark science out of reach (`$53.1520` realized total).
- **Without structured world synthesis and solvability-gated adversary evolution:** Worlds revert to static templates (`results/gemini_roles/world_text/dynamic-events-carbon`, commit `361fbab16b9c1016f72480d4550bb200dc7f5d60`, and `dynamic-events-hard-carbon`, commit `dbe9569fcc44abf8bc0665e0533dd1d1b677c6e9`, add 18 leak-free mid-episode incidents) and scripted moles that fail to challenge tactical baselines (`grok_tactics` drops from `+0.5967` to `+0.0000` only against the live `gemini-4-carbon` mole).
