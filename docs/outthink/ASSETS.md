Notes:
- Kenney tilemaps have no walk cycles. Fake walking with a 2-frame bob or by swapping adjacent character tiles.
- Tile grid is 16px with 1px spacing (Tiny Dungeon, Tiny Town) and 2px (UI sheet). Upscale with nearest-neighbor.
- Download link is signed and needs the kenney.nl cookie plus Referer; plain curl of the page link fails.
- Repo content (docs/gemini/SUBMISSION.md, results/gemini/usage_total.json) was not read for this step.
