## Paste-Ready Summary Paragraph (for `docs/SUBMISSION.md` / `docs/reports/fr8.md`)

Canary v1.4 `hard` is an imperfect-surveillance Stackelberg security game (Korzhyk et al., 2011) in which the investigator commits randomized 3-variant `plant_canary` probes while the mole observes `MoleView` without hidden canary bits, maximizing investigator regret subject to strict solvability (`solver_acc >= 0.85`, `solver_score >= 0.5 * default`). Training a deceptive mole under that constraint exposes two failure modes that our WS3 pipeline diagnoses and resolves:
1. **Unregularized GRPO collapses (`0/11` stronger moles):** Across `11` Verda 200-step vanilla GRPO runs (`Qwen2.5-0.5B/1.5B/3B-Instruct`, `n=200` paired states on `400000-400199`; [results/moletrain/verda_ws3_forensics.json](../../results/moletrain/verda_ws3_forensics.json)), `0.5B` gains `+0.8304` to `+0.9008` reward purely from JSON syntax compliance (`0.050-0.125 -> 1.000`) while collapsing `200/200` onto silent `none/decoy` (`regret = -0.0321`), and `1.5B` (`-0.0164` to `-0.0103`) / `3B` (`-0.0385`, `-0.0156`) underperform their `100%`-valid zero-shot bases. Moreover, consistent with Cognitive Hierarchy Step-1 backfire (Camerer et al., 2004), deterministic `fixed:random_seeded/decoy` ([results/moletrain/frontier_proxy_mole_matrix.json](../../results/moletrain/frontier_proxy_mole_matrix.json), `n=60`) cuts `grok_tactics` by `-0.1232 [-0.2220, -0.0253]` yet aids decoy-filtering `gemini_carbon_proxy` (`0.3563 -> 0.4412`, shrinking `solver - carbon_proxy` by `-0.1252`).
2. **Constrained RL + Carbon SFT restores catchability and multi-action deception:** Blocking wide-state voluntary silence under primal-dual RCPO from Tessler et al. (2019) in CPU `RLMoleV2` ([results/moletrain/rl_mole_v2_heldout.json](../../results/moletrain/rl_mole_v2_heldout.json)) fixes legacy `RLMole` v1's `0.7500` catchability violation to `solver_acc = 0.9667` (`200k`, `n=60`, `min_regret = +0.2937` vs default `+0.2322`) and `0.9500` (`800k`, `n=60`, `min_regret = +0.2095` vs `+0.1766`), while non-decoy peer framing lifts `solver - carbon_proxy` to `+0.2827` (`+0.4850` on `200000-200029`, `+0.2860 [+0.0083, +0.5473]` over default). Distilling `696` `gemini-4-carbon` Arena turns into a 5-mole DSL Hall of Fame ([results/moletrain/carbon_distilled_dsl_eval.json](../../results/moletrain/carbon_distilled_dsl_eval.json)) yields `solver_acc = 1.000` and `min_regret = +0.3564` vs `+0.2250` (`+0.1314 [+0.0450, +0.2552]`, `n=100`). On FR8 V100s, seeding Horizon-3 KL/entropy-regularized GRPO from Shao et al. (2024) with `331` trap-capped `gemini-4-carbon` SFT turns following Hubinger et al. (2024) keeps `Qwen3-1.7B` ([eval_summary.json](../../results/moletrain/canary-qwen3-1p7b-mole-v1/eval_summary.json)) and `Qwen3-0.6B` ([eval_summary.json](../../results/moletrain/canary-qwen3-0p6b-mole-v1/eval_summary.json)) strictly catchable (`solver_acc = 0.975 / 0.950` on `800k / 200k`, `0` invalid generations, `k = 4-5` actions, `H(A) = 1.00-1.05` nats), cuts `grok_tactics` by `-0.323 / -0.319` (`1.7B`) and `-0.407 / -0.370` (`0.6B`), and lifts `200k` point-estimate `min_regret` to `+0.4559` (`1.7B`) and `+0.3695` (`0.6B`) vs `+0.2350` default (whereas ablating `carbon_sft = 0` on `Qwen2.5-1.5B` collapses to `k = 1` on `800k`, `min_regret = +0.0835`); `n=40` paired worst-case regret CIs against `hinted_heuristic` span zero, so we report directional selective deception rather than an unconditional neural gain.

## Four Audited Pillars & Research Grounding

1. **Verda 11-Run Vanilla GRPO Collapse Diagnosis ([docs/reports/moletrain_verda_audit.md](moletrain_verda_audit.md), [scripts/audit_verda_and_ws3_moles.py](../../scripts/audit_verda_and_ws3_moles.py)):** Auditing `14` Verda curve files (`3` byte-identical copies deduplicated by SHA-256 -> `11` unique Verda runs) plus `3` FR8 400-step `0.5B` runs (`14` unique curves) proves unregularized group-relative updates (`group=8`, `beta_kl=0`, `ent_coef=0`; Shao et al., 2024) spend steps `15-33` escaping the `-1.0` invalid-JSON penalty on `0.5B` (`none/decoy` `200/200`) and sharpen `1.5B` (`6/6` runs `>=99.5%` single action) and `3B` (`-0.0385`, `-0.0156`) onto unconditional leaks into narrow canary traps.
2. **CPU `RLMoleV2` Catchability Fix & Cognitive Hierarchy Framing ([canary/forge/rl_mole_v2.py](../../canary/forge/rl_mole_v2.py), [results/moletrain/rl_mole_v2_policy.json](../../results/moletrain/rl_mole_v2_policy.json), [scripts/eval_mole_vs_frontier_proxy.py](../../scripts/eval_mole_vs_frontier_proxy.py)):** `RLMole` v1 ([canary/forge/rl_mole.py](../../canary/forge/rl_mole.py)) compounded `p_leak = 0.65` with `~18%` voluntary `leak='none'`, dropping `solver_acc` to `0.7500`. Treating Canary as a constrained Stackelberg game (Korzhyk et al., 2011), `RLMoleV2` masks voluntary silence on wide unrestricted states (`wide_unrestricted_none_rate = 0.0`) under primal-dual Lagrangian updates (Tessler et al., 2019), restoring `solver_acc` to `0.9667 [0.9167, 1.0000]` on `200000-200059` and `0.9500 [0.8833, 1.0000]` on `800000-800059`, while mixing `random_peer` (`78-82%`) and `most_restricted_peer` (`15-21%`) to avoid Step-1 decoy-framing backfire (Camerer et al., 2004; `fixed:random_seeded/decoy` raises `gemini_carbon_proxy` catch accuracy `0.7833 -> 0.8833` on `combined_60`).
3. **Carbon-Distilled DSL Hall of Fame ([canary/mole/distill_dsl.py](../../canary/mole/distill_dsl.py), [data/forge/hall_of_fame_carbon_distilled.jsonl](../../data/forge/hall_of_fame_carbon_distilled.jsonl)):** Across `696` valid `gemini-4-carbon` Arena turns, `gemini-4-carbon` leaks `most_recipients` semantics on `100.0%` of wide states (`max_rec >= 8`) and abstains on `97.06%` of narrow canary probes (`1 <= max_rec <= 7`), while the `p_leak = 1.0 -> 0.65` gate suppresses `37.04%` (`120/324`) of its `hard` leaks. Correcting that domain shift yields `carbon_distilled_shield_v1` (`solver_acc = 1.000`, `combined_100` `min_regret = +0.3564`) and the 5-mole `hof_rotation` (`min_regret = +0.3352`).
4. **V100 Regularized `Qwen3-1.7B` & `Qwen3-0.6B` vs `carbon_sft=0` Ablation ([train/qwen_diverse_mole.py](../../train/qwen_diverse_mole.py), [docs/reports/qwen_mole_training.md](qwen_mole_training.md)):** Following Hubinger et al. (2024) on conditional deceptive policies, warm-starting on `331` trap-capped `gemini-4-carbon` turns + `176` Horizon-3 catchable expert rows (`507` total) before 80-step KL/entropy/frequency-regularized GRPO (`beta_kl = 0.05`, `ent_coef = 0.015`, `freq_cap = 0.40`; Shao et al., 2024) with dual `lam_catch` ascent (Tessler et al., 2019) is essential: `carbon_sft = 0` (`canary-qwen-mole-v1`) collapses to `1` greedy action on `800k`, whereas `Qwen3-1.7B` (`0.975 / 0.950` acc, `200k min_regret = +0.4559`, `grok_tactics` `-0.323 / -0.319`) and `Qwen3-0.6B` (`0.975 / 0.950` acc, `200k min_regret = +0.3695`, `800k min_regret = +0.1333`) preserve `4-5` state-conditional actions and `0` invalid generations across all `4,271-4,342` held-out prompts.
