## 4. Cross-Artifact Mechanistic Chain: Why Open-Weight Raw-Context Runs Failed While Structured Policies Succeeded

1. **Raw Prompt Header Crowding & Sliding-Window Eviction (`2842/4096` = `69.4%`)**: Tool schemas and system prompt leave `1254` body tokens at `4k`, evicting `97.98%` (`4k`) and `84.65%` (`8k`) of planted canary maps across the `450` audited Verda episodes before `1-3`d (`medium`) and `2-3`d (`hard`) leaks surface, driving `90/94` (`95.7%`) below-breakeven accusations in `v2`.
2. **Off-Policy GRPO Evidence Shift & Inference-Length Confound**: Decision-point GRPO learned culprit reading on clean solver pre-commit states (`0.19 -> 0.892` on `v3-4k-s0`) but planted only `3.53` canaries/ep autonomously (`6/38` precision). Raising inference context to `8k` on the identical `3d0cd43b8037` weights (`WS4-v3-4k-s0-ctx8k`) shifted score by `-0.568` (`-0.716 +/- 0.105`, `70/70` below breakeven). Across `8k` GRPO seeds, `~350`-step A100 runs stayed in the over-accuse regime (`167/171` below breakeven; paired `s3 - 4k@8k = -0.073 +/- 0.173`), while `~650`-step H100 runs suppressed accusations globally (`paired s0 - s3 = +0.728 +/- 0.134`) at the cost of `21/167` missed positive-EV commits.
3. **Action-Serialization `cap=96` Truncation & Implicit Stopping Dilution in Raw DAgger r1**: Each assigned employee in Qwen2.5 `<tool_call>` JSON costs `8` BPE tokens, truncating `133/180` (`73.9%`) of `hard` DAgger `plant_canary` relabels under `max_new_tokens=96`. Even with `max_new_tokens=256`, `250/322` (`77.6%`) of daily states on `TRAIN_SEEDS 108000..108044` lie below breakeven `1/(1+V_known)`, and `159/4000` (`3.98%`) implicit next-token `accuse` labels failed to override `8k` premature commitment (`92%` accused, `-0.542 +/- 0.214` despite `43% -> 68%` own-state agreement and `0.10 -> 0.22` whom-precision). Replacing raw transcripts and JSON action strings with lossless compact sufficient statistics + explicit EV-calibrated breakeven stopping yields verified gains on the strictly separated non-LLM track (`CompactLinearDAggerPolicy` `+0.6267`, `TinyMLPPolicy` `+0.6141`, `EmpiricalBayesPolicy` `+0.6073`).
