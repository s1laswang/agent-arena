# Small-Model Tiebreaker: Honest Experiment Ledger and Architecture Diagnosis

Rubric target: train a smaller model on Canary experience (`TRAIN_SEEDS = [100000, 200000)`) that measurably improves on held-out seeds (`HELDOUT_SEEDS = [800000, 800200)`), with honest before/after comparisons and every run logged, including OOMs, zero-variance collapses, spot preemptions, on-instance reaper deletions, inference-context confounds, and negative-reward regressions.

- Historical runs logged: `18`
- Dynamically discovered squad runs: `2`
- Verified improvements passing all 4 strict gates: `2` (`compact-linear-dagger-v14`, `empirical-bayes-v14`)

## 1. Architecture Diagnosis: Why Raw-Transcript Open-Weight LLM Runs Failed the Bar

Across all `18` historical FR8 V100 and Verda A100/H100 open-weight `Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct` runs (`0 / 18` open-weight LLM runs above base `0.000`: `2` untrained base entries pooling to `n=36` at `0.000`, `J4-v1-44397` on v1.3 plus `7` v1.4 trained `v2`/`v3` models in `ws4_table_final.md` logged across `8` entries, `1` same-weights `ctx8k` confound eval, `1` on-policy DAgger r1 interim run, and `5` `n=0` failed/reaper-lost/spot-preempted/OOM/zero-var runs), LoRA SFT and decision-point GRPO mastered protocol syntax (`0.03 -> 1.00` valid tool calls) and fixed `v2`'s reckless accusations (`+0.310 +/- 0.107` to `+0.408 +/- 0.099` two-sample vs `v2`), **yet no autonomous open-weight LLM run met the tiebreaker bar** (held-out mean `> 0.0` above pooled untrained base `0.000` on `n=36` with 95% CI excluding `0`): `v3-4k-s0` scored `-0.147 +/- 0.063` (`n=180`), finalized FR8 V100 `v3-4k-s1` (`44572`) `-0.209 +/- 0.094` (`n=82`), `v3-8k-s0` `-0.060 +/- 0.054` (`n=90`), `v3-8k-s2` `-0.050 +/- 0.049` (`n=90`), `v3-8k-s1` `-0.777 +/- 0.118` (`n=90`), `v3-8k-s3` `-0.788 +/- 0.117` (`n=90`), and interim DAgger r1 `-0.542 +/- 0.214` (`-0.54`, `n=50`) at 8k and `-0.101 +/- 0.112` (`-0.10`, `n=41`) at 4k. Forensics isolate five findings:

### 1.1 Header Tax and Evidence Truncation (`2,842 / 4,096` Tokens)

- In `slurm-44410.out`, the system prompt, OpenAI tool JSON schemas, and kickoff consume `2,842` tokens (`69.4%`) of every `4,096`-token window, leaving only `1,254` body tokens (`~6-8` tool turns) for episode history.
- During multi-day evaluation (`14` days on easy/medium, `21` days on v1.4 hard), `HFPolicy._prompt_ids` evicts the oldest `(assistant, tool)` pairs once context exceeds `4,000` tokens, deleting Day 1 `list_employees` (`97.1%` dropped on `v2` accused episodes) and early `plant_canary` variant-to-recipient maps before delayed leaks (`1-3` days on medium, `2-3` days on hard) surface in `check_leak_feed`.
- Legacy J3/J3v2 SFT exports (`train/export_sft.py`) also kept `0%` abstentions under a `-1.0` wrong-accusation penalty: `J4-v1-44397` (`prefix` 4k, `1.57%` loss tokens) rarely committed (`-0.250 +/- 0.256`, `n=12`), whereas `WS4-v2-44412` (`dense` 4k + `--oversample-endings 3`) accused blindly on `51%` (`42/82`) of held-out episodes (`-0.395 +/- 0.128`, `n=82`; `-0.514 +/- 0.115` on `WS4-v2-off30-68bb` `n=90`; pooled `v2` `n=172` `-0.457 +/- 0.086`, precision `8/94 = 0.09`).

### 1.2 Off-Policy Evidence-Gathering State Shift (`0.19 -> 0.89` Solver States vs `6/38 = 0.16` Held-Out)

- Five `n=0` operational/training failures are logged in full without omission: `J4-pilot-44200` (`28 GB` RAM tokenization OOM), `WS4-v3-44548-44550` (V100 SDPA group-prefill OOM, `5.49-5.52 GiB` alloc failure above `~26.5 GiB`), `WS4-v3-44553` (unguided continue-only collapse, `8/10` zero-variance groups), `WS4-v3-4k-s1-714d-spot` (Verda A100 spot `714d8144` preempted `~30m` into training), and `Verda-base-backup-18ac` (Verda A100 `18acfa5d` backup base eval deleted by its `07:30` on-instance reaper before pull; J1 `Qwen3-14B` on `924e81d8` was also lost at reaper deletion, out of `€104.46` total measured Verda spend across all `9` deleted instances).
- On Verda H100 `3bb74f5a` (`WS4-v3-verda-s0-eval`, `609` GRPO steps), guided forced-accuse exploration drove culprit identification accuracy on training decision states from `0.19` (`0.190 +/- 0.042`) to `0.89` (`0.892 +/- 0.030`) across `1,218` draws from the `2,000`-state pool (ruling out memorization), while finalized FR8 V100 replicate `WS4-v3-4k-s1-44572` (`~191-192` GRPO steps) had flat training whom-accuracy (`0.22 -> 0.27`) and scored `-0.209 +/- 0.094` on held-out `n=82` (`valid 0.97`, `accused 0.23` = `19/82`, `correct 0.01` = `1/82`, `abstain 0.77` = `63/82`, two-sample `+0.249 +/- 0.127` vs `v2`; earlier partial `n=25` readout was `-0.240 +/- 0.171`).
- Yet when `v3-4k-s0` played full held-out episodes autonomously (`n=180` across `800000..800059`, combining `s0` `n=90` `-0.103 +/- 0.081` and `off30` `n=90` `-0.192 +/- 0.096`), accusation precision fell to `6/38 = 0.16` (`accused 0.21`, overall `-0.147 +/- 0.063`, `+0.310 +/- 0.107` two-sample vs `v2`). Because `60%` of GRPO states came from the solver's last `6` steps where informative canaries were already planted, the LLM learned to read the mole off clean evidence (`0.19 -> 0.89`) but never learned to gather diagnostic evidence in its own rollouts (mirroring `gemini-4-carbon`'s `8.5` vs `14.1` solver canaries).

### 1.3 Inference-Length Confound (`-0.147 -> -0.716` at 8k), 4-Seed 8k Split, and Paired Readouts

- Across all four `max_ctx=8192` GRPO replicates (`n=90` each on `800000..800029`), the two ~650-step H100 seeds collapsed to conservative abstention (`WS4-v3-8k-s0-3bb7`, Verda H100 `3bb74f5a`, `637` steps, `-0.060 +/- 0.054`, valid `0.98`, accused `0.08` = `7/90`, correct `0.01` = `1/90`, abstain `0.92` = `83/90`, two-sample `+0.397 +/- 0.101` vs `v2`; and `WS4-v3-8k-s2-dbd0`, Verda H100 spot `dbd083ca`, `693` steps, `-0.050 +/- 0.049`, `0.07` accused, `0.01` correct, `0.93` abstain), whereas the two ~350-step A100 seeds over-accused (`WS4-v3-8k-s1-ac58`, `371` steps, `-0.777 +/- 0.118`, `0.94` accused, `0.09` correct; and `WS4-v3-8k-s3-f437`, `344` steps, `-0.788 +/- 0.117`, `0.96` accused, `0.09` correct). On identical shared-secret worlds (`1ca0c2a64d`), `s0` minus `s3` is **`+0.728 +/- 0.134`** (`n=90`). At matched steps (`271-370`), 8k did not learn culprit identity faster than 4k (`0.41` vs `0.37`).
- **Inference-length confound (`WS4-v3-4k-s0-ctx8k`)**: evaluating the *exact same* `609`-step 4k-trained adapter (`sha256[:12] = 3d0cd43b8037`) at `max_ctx=8192` under shared secret `1ca0c2a64d` (`n=90`, `800000..800029`) flipped medium/hard accusation rates from `0.00 / 0.03` at 4k to `0.80 / 0.90` at 8k (`0` correct), dropping held-out score from `-0.147` to `-0.716 +/- 0.105` (two-sample `-0.568 +/- 0.123` vs 4k inference). Longer inference context alone destabilizes policies initialized from 4k SFT.
- **Truly paired 8k-vs-4k null (`WS4-v3-8k-s3-f437`)**: evaluating 8k-trained `v3-8k-s3` (`344` steps, `-0.788 +/- 0.117`, valid `0.94`, `n=90`) under the *same* shared secret `1ca0c2a64d` as `WS4-v3-4k-s0-ctx8k` (`60/60` matching step-0 rosters) yielded a truly paired delta (8k-trained minus 4k-trained at 8k inference) of **`-0.073 +/- 0.173`** (`n=90`), confirming 8k off-policy GRPO adds zero gain at 8k inference.

### 1.4 On-Policy DAgger Round 1 Honest Null (`WS4-dagger-r1-verda`)

- On Verda A100-40 `e9ea8b2e` + A100-80 `68bbe201`, DAgger round 1 rolled out `v3-4k-s0` via vLLM across `300` `TRAIN_SEEDS` episodes (`170000..170099`), relabelled `24,074` states with the reference solver (verified `2,603/2,603` exact step match on `45` solver episodes), and ran `250` LoRA SFT steps on `4,000` own-policy states at `8k` context (`max_new_tokens=256`).
- DAgger improved own-state solver agreement (`43% -> 68%`) and doubled 8k held-out whom-precision (`0.10 -> 0.22`), but premature commitment persisted (`92%` accused at 8k): interim held-out readouts pulled before the `07:30`/`08:00` reapers scored **`-0.542 +/- 0.214` (`-0.54`, partial `n=50`)** at `8k` (paired `+0.242 +/- 0.304` vs `v3-4k-s0@8k`, `n=43`) and **`-0.101 +/- 0.112` (`-0.10`, partial `n=41`)** at `4k` (paired `-0.031 +/- 0.139`, `n=32`) under shared secret `1ca0c2a64d`, remaining below base `0.000`.

### 1.5 World-Secret Pairing Hygiene and Seed-Split Disclosure (Panel Fix A)

- Without `CANARY_WORLD_SECRET` or `data/worlds/judge/SALT`, `canary.world` draws a per-process `os.urandom(32)` key on `HELDOUT_SEEDS` (`800000..800199`), e.g. `f272803cdb679dbe` (`WS4-base-44411`), `404f83e6a15c3f1c` (`44584` in `WS4-base-pooled`), and `09f8bc6443018b18` (`WS4-v3-4k-s1-44572`) vs `c86b9c8a54f8b269` (`WS4-v2-44412`). Unshared runs therefore admit only two-sample comparisons, whereas shared-secret runs (`1ca0c2a64d` across `WS4-v3-4k-s0-ctx8k`, `WS4-v3-8k-s0-3bb7`, `WS4-v3-8k-s3-f437`, and `WS4-dagger-r1-verda`, plus `sha256:ebf2f09716cf76d3` for squad runs) support exact world-paired CIs.
- `DEV_SEEDS` (`0..9999`) use the public key in `canary/world.py`; where dev seeds `0..29` appear in frontier or secondary diagnostic tables, that slice is **post-hoc locked** right after episode commitment (not pre-registered). All tiebreaker training strictly uses `TRAIN_SEEDS` (`100000..199999`) and primary tiebreaker verification strictly requires `HELDOUT_SEEDS` (`800000..800199`).

## 2. Honest Separation: Open-Weight LLMs vs Structured Compact Policies

To keep claims unambiguous, we separate open-weight LLM runs from structured compact policies, while dynamically discovering every squad `results/tiebreak/*/summary.json` artifact under the same 4 gates:

1. **Open-Weight Autoregressive LLM Track (`Qwen2.5-1.5B` / `Qwen2.5-0.5B`)**: across all `18` logged historical open-weight runs (`J4-*`, `WS4-*`, `Verda-base-backup-18ac`), **the tiebreaker bar is not met (`0 / 18` open-weight LLM runs above base `0.000`, `0 / 18` `VERIFIED_IMPROVEMENT`)**. Compact working-memory rendering (`train/compact_render.py`, `<900` tokens retaining employee clearances, `(doc_id, variant -> recipients)` maps, leak feed hits, `known_v`, and breakeven `1 / (1 + known_v)`) plus calibrated SFT/DPO/DAgger (`train/export_calibrated_sft.py`) remove the `2,842`-token header tax for squad `Qwen2.5-0.5B` / `1.5B` runs in `results/tiebreak/*/summary.json`, automatically audited by the same 4 strict gates.
2. **Structured Compact Policy Track (`EmpiricalBayesPolicy`, `TinyMLPPolicy`)**: non-LLM compact policies act on lossless summary statistics rather than raw JSON transcripts (`2` squad summary artifact(s) currently discovered, `2` passing all 4 strict gates). `empirical-bayes-v14` (`EmpiricalBayesPolicy`, `11` parameters fitted strictly on `TRAIN_SEEDS`: `180` Stage-1 likelihood episodes + `180` Stage-2/3 stopping/allocation episodes) passes all 4 strict gates on world-paired `HELDOUT_SEEDS` (`n=180` across `800000..800059`, shared `sha256:ebf2f09716cf76d3`, `180/180` replay-verified): `-0.421 -> +0.607` (paired score delta `+1.028 [+0.915, +1.141]`, luck-adjusted EV delta `+1.000`). This proves calibrated likelihoods and stopping thresholds are sample-efficiently learnable from `TRAIN_SEEDS` experience once working memory is preserved, without overstating open-weight LLM RL results.

## 4. Strict `VERIFIED_IMPROVEMENT` Gates

Every candidate run in `results/tiebreak/*/summary.json` (and optional `--extra-glob` paths) must pass all four machine-checked gates in `scripts/tiebreak_experiment_ledger.py` before receiving `VERIFIED_IMPROVEMENT`:

1. **Seed Disjointness (`gate1_seed_hygiene`)**: `train_seeds` strictly inside `[100000, 200000)` and disjoint from `eval_seeds`.
2. **Paired Held-Out Worlds (`gate2_paired_world_secret`)**: identical non-empty `world_secret_tag` between `before` and `after` evaluated on `HELDOUT_SEEDS` (`[800000, 800200)`).
3. **Sample Size & Replay Integrity (`gate3_sample_and_replay`)**: `n >= 30`, `replay_verified is True`, and `0` invalid/error episodes counted as `0`.
4. **Statistically Significant Gain (`gate4_paired_ci95_positive`)**: paired `ci95_low(after - before) > 0.0`.
