Case brief

One of twenty employees is leaking company documents. Find them before the value runs out.

You run security. Every leak costs the company value, and leaks only show up in the press a few days later. You can read access logs, search internal messages, interview staff, restrict access, and plant canaries: copies of one document that differ per group of recipients, so a leaked copy tells you which group it came from. You get exactly one accusation, and a wrong one costs more than doing nothing.

  1. 01
    Map who holds what. Staff, documents and recipients, day by day.
  2. 02
    Set traps. Split suspects into variants A, B and C, then watch the leak feed.
  3. 03
    Accuse once. A correct accusation scores the value left. A wrong one scores −1.

This page runs the real benchmark environment (the canary Python package) in your browser. The rules you can open are word for word what the AI agents are given, and no tool runs unless you press its button.
