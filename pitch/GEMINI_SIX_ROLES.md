**3. What is innovative.**
- **Difficulty by reasoning, never unfairness:** PAIRED scenario proposals (`paired-designer-carbon-hard`, commit `9222669912afd57e53fbd3b07f0cdba985df705d`, 6/6 kept) are accepted only when `SolverPolicy` stays high and heuristics fail, guaranteeing solvable hardness.
- **Thinking-level regret attribution:** On `hard` seed `0` (`results/gemini_roles/investigators/thinking-ladder-carbon-hard0`, commit `3df4d2b65bd05a67f2d94ef18d967da05c0e90d7`), scaling `gemini-4-carbon` from `0` to `52,968` thinking tokens cuts step-level Bayesian regret by `81.6%` (`0.1623 -> 0.0298`) and eliminates `uninformative_canary` errors while isolating over-cautious commit timing.
- **Checked, not trusted:** Every Failure Analyst quote (`16/16`) is verified verbatim in Python, every regret tag comes from `SolverPolicy`, and every Exploit Hunter score comes from the server-side ledger.
