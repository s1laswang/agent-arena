## 3. What Is Innovative

- **Difficulty by reasoning, never unfairness:** PAIRED scenario proposals and Mole Forge children are accepted only when `SolverPolicy` stays high and naive heuristics fail, guaranteeing catchable hardness.
- **Thinking-level Bayesian regret attribution:** Controlled `low` vs `high` `thinking_level` runs on identical `gemini-4-carbon` weights and world seeds separate experimental-design gains from stopping conservatism.
- **Checked, not trusted:** Every `failure_analyst` quote is verified verbatim in Python, every regret tag comes from `SolverPolicy`, every `multimodal_evidence` PNG watermark readout (`Part.from_bytes`) is paired against blind and text-oracle controls, and every `exploit_hunter` score comes strictly from the server-side ledger.

## 4. Why Gemini Is Essential

- **Controlled `thinking_level` on one model:** Holding `gemini-4-carbon`, tool schemas, and `v1.4` seeds fixed in `results/gemini/roles_carbon/investigators/thinking-ladder-carbon-hard0` (commit `3df4d2b65bd05a67f2d94ef18d967da05c0e90d7`) isolates reasoning depth as a single controlled variable (`52,968` thinking tokens, `low +0.780 (0 think tok, day 7) -> high +0.360 (52,968 think tok, day 13) vs solver +0.640`).
- **Load-bearing `thought_signature` replay:** Preserving cryptographic `thought_signature` tokens across `3,649` multi-turn 13-tool investigator calls and `7,994` total suite calls keeps latent Bayesian hypothesis state intact across multi-day tool invocations; stripping signatures causes immediate `400 INVALID_ARGUMENT` rejection on Gemini function-call replay (`canary.gemini.ablations`).
- **Explicit and prefix context caching:** Serving **209,114,093** of **229,183,059** input tokens from explicit and prefix cache (**91.2%** cache hit rate across **7,994** calls) keeps effective API spend at **$100.0162** (vs **$503.2073** uncached list price).
- **1M-token long context:** Ingests whole-repository whitebox security packs (`104` files, `203,463` tokens) and `1,114,554` failure-analyst input tokens in unfragmented single-prompt passes without lossy RAG chunking, while every cited quote and regret delta is checked deterministically in Python.
- **Native multimodal vision (`Part.from_bytes`):** Reads `260` rendered PNG scans, dossiers, SOC heatmaps, and belief charts directly via `Part.from_bytes(mime_type="image/png")`; across `n=27` unique redacted-memo episodes (`140` PNG scans), vision lifts score from `+0.1822` (blind) to `+0.6263` (`+0.4441 +/- 0.1219` 95% CI, `100.0%` watermark accuracy, `100.0%` Bayesian text-oracle recovery).
